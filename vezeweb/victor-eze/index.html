01 About me

I like turning problems into products.

I'm a software developer and product builder from Nigeria. My work sits somewhere between technology, design and entrepreneurship.

I started by building websites and digital experiences for businesses. Over time, I became increasingly interested in what happens beyond the website — understanding the problem, building the product and seeing how people actually use it.

That shift has led me from client work through Vezeweb to building products of my own, including Vezstora.

Why I build

Technology should make things easier.

I've always been interested in what happens when technology removes friction from something people already want to do.

While working with businesses, I noticed how often a company's online presence could become more complicated than the actual problem it was supposed to solve.

That experience shaped how I think about products. I don't want to build software simply because something can be built. I want to understand what the person using it is trying to accomplish and find a simpler way to help them get there.

That's the thinking behind both my work through Vezeweb and the products I'm building today.

The work

Building with Vezeweb.

Vezeweb is the web development and digital product side of my work.

I work with businesses to create websites and digital experiences that help them communicate clearly, build trust and give their customers a better way to interact with them online.

But for me, the goal has never been to simply deliver another website. The interesting question is always: what does the business actually need the website to accomplish?

Vezstora

Product building

From building websites to building a product.

Vezstora is a SaaS platform I founded to help small businesses create and manage their own online storefronts.

The idea came from seeing businesses rely heavily on Instagram and WhatsApp to sell their products, while customers had to ask questions through DMs before they could even place an order.

I wanted to create something simpler — a platform that gives a business its own online storefront without requiring the owner to understand web development.
